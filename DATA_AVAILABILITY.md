# Data Availability

This page describes the availability and conditions of use for the data associated with the study:

**Network-Extended Theory of Planned Behavior for Digital Collaboration in Construction Project Management**

## Availability of Study Data

The respondent-level survey dataset used in this study is **not provided as an unrestricted public-use dataset**. The data are the property of their respective owners and were used with prior permission.

Any use of the dataset in future research or other work requires prior approval from the corresponding author:

**Dr. Long D. Nguyen**  
Florida Gulf Coast University  
Email: lnguyen@fgcu.edu

Users who receive permission to use the dataset must provide appropriate attribution and citation in all resulting publications or materials.

## Publicly Available Reproducibility Materials

The analysis code and Jupyter Notebooks used to reproduce the reported statistical analyses are publicly available in this GitHub repository.

For the current analysis notebook, robustness checks, and supplementary analyses, see:

**[Supplemental Material](./SUPPLEMENTAL_MATERIAL.md)**

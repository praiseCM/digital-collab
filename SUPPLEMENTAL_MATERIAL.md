# Supplemental Material

This page identifies the supplemental and reproducibility materials associated with the study:

**Network-Extended Theory of Planned Behavior for Digital Collaboration in Construction Project Management**

## Current Reproducibility Notebook

### `network_extended_tpb_v02.ipynb`

This is the **current notebook corresponding to the revised manuscript**.

It contains two clearly separated parts:

- **Part A — Primary Manuscript Analyses**  
  Reproduces the analyses, statistical results, Tables 1–6, and Figures 1–2 reported in the revised manuscript.

- **Part B — Supplementary Robustness and Sensitivity Analyses**  
  Provides additional analyses used to evaluate construct overlap, measurement-model sensitivity, shared variance among predictors, professional-role sensitivity, common-method concerns, and alternative mediation specifications.
